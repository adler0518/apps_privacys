Privacy Policy

InkRetouch · Last updated: August 7, 2026

InkRetouch ("we", "the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

1. On-device text detection

Detecting the text inside your image (OCR) runs entirely on your device. No image is uploaded in order to detect text.

2. AI text editing (cloud)

When you tap a detected text area and confirm a replacement, the image you are currently editing is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to Alibaba Cloud Model Studio for that single AI edit. The returned result is used to replace your image. We do not otherwise retain, analyze, or use it for model training.

3. What we do not collect

We do not collect, store, or distribute your original images or personal information. There are no user accounts and no login. Camera and photo library permissions are used only for imports and saves that you initiate.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases (credit packs) are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment information.

5. Network use

Apart from the AI text-editing step described above, the app has no other networking features and no advertising or tracking SDKs.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
