Heartbeat Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 27, 2026

Welcome to Heartbeat. We value your privacy and personal data security. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when using our app.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide: Nickname, avatar, chat content with virtual characters, persona customization text.

Device and usage information: Device model, OS version, language and timezone, app launch count, daily chat count, crash logs (without chat content).

Information we do NOT collect: We do not collect contacts, location, call history, or SMS content. No third-party advertising SDKs.

2. How We Use Information

Chat content and memory facts provide coherent dialogue experiences. Quota information ensures feature boundaries. Crash logs help fix issues.

Chat records are stored locally on your device. Not uploaded unless cloud sync is explicitly enabled.

3. Information Storage and Protection

Local storage uses iOS UserDefaults and Keychain. Network requests via HTTPS encryption. We do not sell or provide your information to third parties.

4. Children Protection

This app is designed for users aged 17+. Built-in safety guards intervene when sensitive content is detected.

5. Your Rights

Clear conversations anytime. Delete app to clear all local data. Contact us to access, correct, or delete information.

6. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated. Significant changes will be notified in-app.
