Privacy Policy — Photo Revive (老照片修复馆)

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Photo Revive ("the App") restores old photographs using AI. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to your data. Short version: we collect as little as technically possible, and your photos are deleted right after processing.

1. Photos you choose to restore

When you confirm a restoration, the selected photo is uploaded over an encrypted connection to our AI restoration service for processing. Once the restored result is returned to your device, the uploaded file is deleted from the processing server. Your photos are:

  • Uploaded only after you explicitly confirm each restoration
  • Used solely to produce your restored image
  • Deleted immediately after processing
  • Never used to train or improve AI models
  • Never shared with or sold to third parties

2. Face data

Restoring old family photos involves enhancing faces that appear in them. Before your first restoration, the App asks for your explicit consent to process facial information in your photos. We do not extract, store, or index facial feature data; faces are enhanced only as part of the image itself, and the image is deleted after processing as described above. The App does not offer face swapping or face reshaping.

3. What we do NOT collect

  • No accounts, names, email addresses or phone numbers
  • No location data
  • No advertising identifiers; no advertising SDKs; no ads
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No contacts, health data or any other personal data

4. Purchases

Credit packs are sold through Apple In-App Purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see or store your payment information. Purchase records are managed by Apple and can be viewed in your Apple ID account. Credits are counted locally on your device.

5. Data stored on your device

The App stores your remaining credit count and preference settings locally (Keychain / UserDefaults). Your photo library is never modified; restored images are saved only when you choose to save them. Uninstalling the App removes locally stored preferences.

6. AI-generated content labeling

Each restored image is written with an "AI-restored" metadata label, in line with deep-synthesis content labeling requirements, so restored results are not mistaken for untouched originals.

7. Children

The App does not collect personal data from anyone, including children. It is intended for restoring family photographs that you own or are authorized to use.

8. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new date before the change takes effect.
