Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-11 · App: Sticker Meme Studio (表情包工厂)

1. Introduction

Sticker Meme Studio helps users create chat reactions, animated replies and meme images from portraits, role templates and text prompts. This policy explains what information is handled when you use the app.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not create user accounts, do not collect location, contacts, financial information, advertising identifiers or browsing history, and do not use data for third-party tracking.

3. Photos, Prompts and Generated Content

When you choose a photo or save generated content, the app uses the iOS Photos permission only for the feature you request. Photos and generated images are stored on your device unless you choose to share them.

4. AI Generation Service

If AI generation is configured and you actively request generation, selected photos and prompts may be transmitted to the configured generation service solely to create the requested sticker, reaction or meme result. The app does not use this content for advertising tracking.

5. Data Stored on Your Device

Generated images, videos and exported content may be saved to your Photos library only after your action or permission. Deleting the app removes app-local data. Content saved to Photos can be deleted from the Photos app.

6. Permissions

The app requests photo library access to let you pick a portrait and save generated reactions, memes and sticker content. You can deny or revoke permission in iOS Settings.

7. Data Security

We use the system security model provided by iOS. Please avoid uploading images that contain sensitive personal information if you do not want them processed by the generation service.

8. Children's Privacy

The app is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when app features change. The latest version will be available at this page.

10. Contact Us

For privacy questions, contact: zyl04401@163.com
