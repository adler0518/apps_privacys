About the App

Sticker Meme Studio helps you create chat reactions, animated replies and shareable meme images from portraits, role templates and text prompts.

Common Questions

Why does the app ask for Photos permission?
The app uses Photos access only when you choose a portrait or save generated sticker, reaction or meme content to your photo library.

Can I use the app without uploading my own photo?
Yes. You can start with built-in role templates and create sticker or meme content from those templates.

Where are generated images saved?
Generated content can be saved to your iOS Photos library when you tap the save action.
