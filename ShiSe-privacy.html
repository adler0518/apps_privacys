Privacy Policy - ShiSe

Last Updated: August 22, 2026

Overview

ShiSe ("we", "the app") is a color palette extraction tool. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how the app handles your data.

Data Collection

ShiSe does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

  • No personal information is collected
  • No usage analytics or tracking
  • No third-party SDKs or advertising
  • No network requests for core functionality
  • No data leaves your device

Camera Permission

The app requests camera access solely for real-time color extraction. Camera frames are processed entirely on-device using local computation (CIELAB color space analysis and K-Means++ clustering). No images or video are recorded, stored, or transmitted.

Photo Library Access

The app requests write-only access to your photo library to save generated palette card images. The app never reads your existing photos.

Data Storage

All extracted color palettes and generated images are stored locally on your device. You can delete them at any time through the app or by uninstalling the app.

Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share any user data with third parties. There is no data to share.

Children's Privacy

The app does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Your Rights

Since no data is collected, there is no data to access, correct, or delete from our servers. All app data resides solely on your device and can be removed by uninstalling the app.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will post the new version at this URL with an updated revision date.
