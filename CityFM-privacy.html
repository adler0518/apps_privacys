Privacy Policy

CityFM: World Radio & Weather — Last updated: July 28, 2026

Overview

CityFM ("the App") respects your privacy. The App is designed to work without collecting any personal data. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information.

Data Collection

The App does not collect any data. Specifically:

  • No account or registration is required.
  • No personal information (name, email, phone number) is requested or stored.
  • No location data is accessed. City selection is entirely manual through search.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs are integrated.
  • No device identifiers are collected.

Local Storage

Your preferences (interface language, temperature unit) and your saved cities and favorite radio stations are stored only on your device. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

Third-Party Services

The App requests publicly available data from the following services to provide its core features. These requests contain only the city name or coordinates you search for, and no personal identifiers:

  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) — city geocoding and weather forecasts.
  • Nominatim / OpenStreetMap (openstreetmap.org) — fallback city search.
  • Radio Browser (radio-browser.info) — community radio station directory and audio stream URLs.

Audio streams are provided by the individual radio broadcasters. Standard network connection information (such as IP address) may be technically visible to these services when streaming, as with any internet connection. The App itself does not log or store this information.

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If our privacy practices change, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
