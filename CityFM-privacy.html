Privacy Policy for City FM

Last updated: July 27, 2026

City FM is a free radio and podcast player for iOS. It lets you stream live city radio stations, browse channels and keep a list of your favorites. We have built the app so that you can enjoy listening without giving up any personal information. This policy explains, in plain terms, exactly what happens to your data.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

  • City FM does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide a name, email address, phone number, or any other identifier.
  • We do not collect your precise or approximate location.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, calendar, health data, or advertising identifier.
  • The app contains no third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs, and we do not build a profile of your listening habits for sale or ad targeting.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

  • Favorites: the channels and podcasts you save are stored only in local storage on your own device.
  • Playback state: your last played channel and playback position are kept locally so you can resume where you left off.

This information never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us. Deleting the app permanently removes all of it.

3. Streaming and Network Activity

When you play a station, City FM connects to that station's publicly available audio stream. As with any internet request, the stream provider's server automatically receives standard technical data such as your IP address and a request timestamp. This is required for the stream to reach you; we do not operate these servers and do not receive this data ourselves.

4. Third-Party Services

Radio streams and podcast feeds are operated by their respective broadcasters. City FM does not embed third-party SDKs and does not share any data with third parties, because we do not hold any personal data to share. A broadcaster's own handling of standard server-log data is governed by that broadcaster's policies.

5. Children's Privacy

City FM does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). Because the app collects no personal data at all, there is no such data to delete.

6. Your Rights

Because all app data stays on your device, you are always in full control: you can remove individual favorites inside the app, or delete everything at any time by removing the app from your device.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of City FM after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
